Bunny Playground

Nine rounds. One giant rabbit. Follow the floor arrows to the green EXIT door.
The rabbit runs as fast as you, so duck into a yellow room off the side of the maze: it waits at the door you used, then wanders off after 20 seconds. Every room has two doors.

Each round starts with a 5-note melody. Remember it: at the exit door you must pick it out from three choices, or the round restarts.
